# Bundled sprite attribution

Beatrice by legeling, from https://github.com/legeling/awesome-codex-pet/tree/main/pets/beatrice--legeling. MIT license retained in `../beatrice-legeling/original/LICENSE`. Animation strips are cropped from the original sheet.

Hornet is from Hollow Knight: Silksong by Team Cherry, via the sprite pack at https://ilves15.itch.io/hollow-knight-silksong-sprites. Frames are re-aligned to a shared 512 x 280 canvas. The art belongs to Team Cherry; it is included here as fan use, not under an open license.

Other personal pets with unspecified licenses are not bundled. Upload your own sheets in Settings.
